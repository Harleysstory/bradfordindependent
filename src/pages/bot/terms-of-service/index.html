What the bot does

The bot relays activity notifications from GitHub repositories — commits, pull requests, issues, releases and similar events — into Discord channels chosen by the server's administrators. It works only with repositories that a repository owner or administrator has deliberately connected to it, and only posts where it has been configured to post.
