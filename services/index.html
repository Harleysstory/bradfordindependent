Services organised around the problem, not the job title.

Business analysis comes first — everything else exists to support it. Here's what each service involves, when you might need it, and what you actually receive.

Primary service

Business Analysis

The foundation of everything else I do.

What it is: understanding how an organisation actually works — its processes, systems, services and the relationships between them. Not how the org chart says it works; how it really works on a Tuesday afternoon.

When you might need it

  • You're about to change something and don't have a clear picture of the current state
  • The same problems keep resurfacing no matter what you try
  • Different teams describe "the process" in completely different ways
  • Knowledge about how things work lives in a few people's heads

What I do

  • Process mapping — how work actually flows, end to end
  • Systems analysis — how tools, data and processes connect
  • Stakeholder analysis — who is affected, and how
  • Talking to the people who do the work, not just those who manage it

What you receive

  • Clear process maps and a written picture of how things work today
  • Identified bottlenecks, inefficiencies and dependencies
  • Prioritised, practical recommendations
  • Documentation your team can actually use afterwards

Process & Systems Improvement

Making the work flow the way it should.

What it is: taking what the analysis reveals and redesigning how work gets done — removing friction, duplication and handoffs that add risk without adding value.

When you might need it

  • Work consistently takes longer than it should
  • The same issues keep appearing in the same places
  • Workarounds have become the normal way of operating
  • Growth has added friction to everything

What I do

  • Bottleneck and inefficiency analysis
  • Redesigning processes around how work actually happens
  • Removing duplication and unnecessary handoffs
  • Aligning systems with the way people actually work

What you receive

  • A current-state versus improved-process comparison
  • Specific changes, ranked by impact and effort
  • Support putting those changes in place

Service Design & UX

Improving services from the user's point of view.

What it is: redesigning services and experiences so they work better for the people using them — and simpler to deliver for the organisation providing them.

When you might need it

  • Users are confused, complain, or give up partway through
  • A service has grown by accretion and nobody designed it end to end
  • Your team is too close to the service to see it clearly

What I do

  • User journey mapping and insight gathering
  • Redesigning touchpoints and service steps
  • Making services accessible and easier to understand
  • Testing and refining with real feedback

What you receive

  • Journey maps showing where the experience breaks down
  • Redesigned service steps and touchpoints
  • Clear, accessible materials and communications
  • Recommendations grounded in evidence, not taste

Digital Systems & Development

Building the digital side — on a correct understanding of the problem.

What it is: designing and building websites, workflows and digital systems. The difference here: the build is preceded by analysis, so you're not paying to digitalise a broken process.

When you might need it

  • A process needs a proper system, not another spreadsheet
  • Your website needs rebuilding — or finally building properly
  • Your tools don't fit how your organisation actually works

What I do

  • Requirements and specification — the business analysis comes first
  • Building with appropriate tools, including WordPress and custom CSS where that's the right choice
  • Implementing workflows and documenting them
  • Honest advice on what's worth building — and what isn't

What you receive

  • A working system, workflow or website
  • Documentation and a proper handover
  • Specifications you could hand to any developer later

Strategy & Growth

Turning direction into a plan an organisation can follow.

What it is: helping founders, startups, charities and community organisations turn ideas into clear strategies and measurable action plans — especially when the organisation's structure needs to grow up alongside its ambition.

When you might need it

  • You have lots of ideas and no order of attack
  • You're planning growth or a restructure and need clarity first
  • You need to align people behind a plan before committing resource

What I do

  • Organisational development and engagement initiatives
  • Growth planning and operational restructuring
  • Turning strategy into specific, measurable actions

What you receive

  • A clear strategy written in plain English
  • A realistic action plan with owners and sequence
  • Support communicating and implementing it
Not sure which one you need? That's normal — and it's exactly the kind of question a first conversation settles. Start a conversation and describe what's happening; I'll tell you honestly whether analysis, improvement, build — or something else entirely — is what you need.
